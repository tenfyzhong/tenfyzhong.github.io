# Category and Tag Usage Summary

This document summarizes the usage of categories and tags in the blog posts under `content/posts/`.

## Categories

Categories are used to group posts into broad topics. Each post typically belongs to one category.

Here are the categories currently in use:

*   **网络技术**: Posts related to network technology, such as DNS, TCP/IP, network topology, etc.
*   **工具**: Posts about tools and utilities that improve productivity, such as `dash`, `fish-shell`, `homebrew`, etc.
*   **编程语言**: Posts focusing on specific programming languages like `golang`.
*   **运维**: Posts about DevOps, server administration, and related topics, including `docker`, `nginx`, `prometheus`, etc.
*   **人工智能**: Posts related to Artificial Intelligence, including `AI`, `deepseek`, `gemini`, etc.
*   **操作系统**: Posts about operating systems, mainly `linux` and `macos`.
*   **数据库**: Posts related to databases, such as `mongodb`, `redis`.
*   **后端开发**: Posts about backend development concepts and algorithms.
*   **Vim**: Posts specifically about the Vim editor.

## Tags

Tags are used to describe the specific topics of a post in more detail. A post can have multiple tags. Tags are always in lowercase English.

Here is a list of some of the tags used and their context:

*   **network**: Used for posts related to networking in general.
*   **dns**: For posts about the Domain Name System.
*   **cloudflare**: Specific to Cloudflare services.
*   **tutorial**: For posts that are tutorials or how-to guides.
*   **linux**: For posts related to the Linux operating system.
*   **golang**: For posts about the Go programming language.
*   **tools**: For posts that introduce a tool or utility.
*   **devops**: For posts related to DevOps practices.
*   **ai**: For posts about Artificial Intelligence.
*   **git**: For posts related to the git version control system.
*   **cli**: For command-line interface tools.
*   **vim**: For posts about the Vim editor.
*   **database**: For posts about databases.
*   **performance**: For posts discussing performance tuning and benchmarking.
*   **security**: For posts related to security.

## Usage Patterns and Conventions

*   **Categories are singular and in Chinese**: Each post has one primary category that defines its main topic.
*   **Tags are multiple and in English**: Tags are more granular and a post can have several tags to describe its content in detail. They are always in lowercase.
*   **Consistency**: There is a consistent use of categories and tags across the posts, which helps in organizing and finding content. For example, a post about a networking tool will have the category "网络技术" and tags like "network" and the specific tool's name.
